# Contributions

Thank you for your interest. **External code contributions are not accepted at present.** Pull requests are automatically closed without merging. Please do not submit patches or grant rights through a contribution agreement.

You can study and fork the source under AGPL-3.0-only. Forks must use their own product name and identifying artwork as explained in TRADEMARKS.md. Report security issues privately using SECURITY.md.

Closing contributions preserves the maintainer's control of future licensing of their own work; it does not remove rights already granted under the AGPL for published versions or grant rights over third-party components.
